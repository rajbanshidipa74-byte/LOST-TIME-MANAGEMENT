### 2. Business Need

Many people struggle to manage their daily activities effectively. They often spend time on unnecessary activities and find it difficult to identify where their time is being wasted.

Without proper planning and activity tracking, users may fail to complete important tasks and achieve their daily goals.

Therefore, a Lost Time Management System is proposed to help users monitor their daily activities, identify unproductive time, and improve their time management skills.

### 3. Business Requirements

The proposed system should provide the following functionalities:

1. **Account Management:** Allow users to register, log in, and log out securely.
2. **Dashboard:** Display daily activity summaries and tracked-time information.
3. **Activity Tracking:** Allow users to record and monitor their daily activities.
4. **Daily Planner:** Allow users to create, organize, and manage daily tasks.
5. **Statistics and Lost-Time Analysis:** Analyze productive and unproductive time and display useful statistics.
6. **Profile and Goal Settings:** Allow users to manage personal information and set daily goals.
7. **Email Notifications:** Provide email notifications for relevant system activities and reminders.

### 4. Business Value

The Lost Time Management System is expected to provide the following benefits:

- Helps users understand how they spend their time.
- Improves daily planning and time management.
- Helps identify unproductive activities.
- Encourages users to achieve their daily goals.
- Increases personal productivity.
- Provides useful statistics about daily activities.

### 5. Special Issues and Constraints

The project may face the following challenges:

- Limited development time due to academic schedules.
- Different technical skill levels among team members.
- Possible technical difficulties during system integration.
- The system requires reliable data storage and secure authentication.
- Users need an easy-to-understand interface.
- The development team must complete the project within the university submission deadline.

### 6. Expected Outcome

The expected outcome is a functional web-based Lost Time Management System that allows users to manage their accounts, track activities, plan daily tasks, analyze lost time, and improve their productivity.

### 7. Conclusion

The Lost Time Management System is proposed as a solution to help people manage their daily activities more effectively.

By providing activity tracking, daily planning, time analysis, and goal-setting features, the system aims to help users understand their time usage and make better use of their time.
